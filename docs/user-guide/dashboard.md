# Your dashboard

**Who this is for:** every signed-in StoreCart user.

The dashboard is the first page you see after you sign in. It summarises your account and links to
everything you can manage today.

> **Status:** the dashboard is about your **account** for now. Your organisation, stores, products
> and orders will appear here once they are built. See [What's coming](roadmap.md).

## Get around

The bar at the top of every dashboard page has these links:

| Link | Takes you to |
|---|---|
| **Home** | The dashboard overview |
| **Profile** | Your name, bio and contact details ([guide](profile-and-settings.md#edit-your-profile)) |
| **Settings** | Theme, email notifications, language and timezone ([guide](profile-and-settings.md#change-your-preferences)) |
| **Security** | Password, two-factor authentication, login history, account deletion ([guide](profile-and-settings.md#security)) |
| **Billing** | Your plan, payment methods and invoices ([guide](plans-and-billing.md)) |

Select your avatar in the top-right corner for quick links to **Dashboard**, **Profile**,
**Security** and **Log out**.

On small screens the top links are hidden. Use the avatar menu, or the **Quick Actions** cards on
the overview.

## What the overview shows

### Upgrade banner

If you are on the Free plan, an **Upgrade to Pro** banner appears at the top. Select
**Upgrade Now** to see the plans. See [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md).

### Summary cards

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Account Status** | **Verified** once you have verified your email. |
| **Subscription Plan** | Your current plan (Free, Pro or Enterprise). For a paid plan, the date it is active until. |
| **Notifications** | Your unread notification count. **Being removed**; see below. |
| **Total Logins** | How many times you have signed in successfully. |

### Quick Actions

Shortcut cards to **Edit Profile**, **Settings**, **Security** and **Billing**.

### Account Information

Your email, name, the date you joined (**Member Since**) and your **Last Login**. Select
**Edit Profile** to change your details.

### Recent Activity

Your last five sign-ins, with the date and time, IP address, location (when known) and whether
the sign-in was a **Success** or **Failed**.

If you see a sign-in you don't recognise:

1. [Change your password](profile-and-settings.md#change-your-password) straight away.
2. Turn on [two-factor authentication](profile-and-settings.md#two-factor-authentication-2fa).

The full list is on the **Security** page under
[Login history](profile-and-settings.md#review-your-login-history).

### Account Summary

Totals for your account: **Account Age** in days, **Total Logins**, **Notifications** and your
**Current Plan**.

## After you upgrade

When you return from checkout after buying a plan, the dashboard shows "Processing Your
Subscription..." for a few seconds, then "Subscription Activated!" and refreshes. Your new plan
then appears in the **Subscription Plan** card.

## Notifications (being removed)

You may see a bell icon in the top bar, a **Notifications** card and a **Recent Notifications**
list. In-app notifications are **being removed** from this release and will return in a redesigned
form later. Don't rely on them for anything important. See [What's coming](roadmap.md#removed-for-redesign).

## Account statistics

The counts on the overview (total logins, account age, notification totals) **may change** or move
during the rebuild.
