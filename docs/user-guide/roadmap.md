# What's coming

**Who this is for:** everyone who wants to know what StoreCart will do next.

> **Status:** StoreCart is being rebuilt on a new foundation. This page describes plans, not
> promises. Dates aren't given, and features may change before they launch.

## Available now

- Create an account, verify your email, sign in and out, reset your password
  ([Getting started](getting-started.md)).
- Edit your profile and preferences, change your password, use two-factor authentication and
  review your login history ([Profile, settings and security](profile-and-settings.md)).
- Choose a plan, upgrade, cancel or resume, manage cards and download invoices
  ([Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md)).
- A minimal admin area for StoreCart staff ([Admin guide](admin-guide.md)).

## Coming soon: the commerce features

None of these is available yet.

### Organisations and teams

- Create an **organisation** for your business.
- Invite team members and give them a role: **Owner**, **Admin** or **Staff**.
- Your subscription will belong to the organisation and cover all its stores.

### Stores

- Open **several stores** under one organisation.
- Each store keeps its own data separate from every other store.
- A guided set-up for your first store.

### Catalogue

- Add products to each store, with descriptions, prices and images.

### Storefront and cart

- Each store gets its own **storefront** on its own web address, with a shopping cart and
  checkout.
- Your own **custom domain** comes later.

### Orders and payments

- Customers pay you directly through Stripe, with you as the seller.
- View and manage **orders** for each store.
- Shipping, tax and store limits follow.

### Later

After the first commerce release, the plan includes discounts, tax zones, customer accounts for
shoppers, returns and store analytics. Further ahead: a marketplace, a mobile app and a public API.

## Removed for redesign

These features exist in the current app but are **being removed** from this release. They will
return later, redesigned for organisations and stores. Until then, don't rely on them, even where
you can still see them on screen.

| Feature | Where you may see it today | What's planned |
|---|---|---|
| **In-app notifications** | Bell icon, Notifications cards on the dashboard | Redesigned notifications |
| **Data export** | **Export My Data** on the Security page | Redesigned data export |
| **SEO meta tags and URL redirects** | Admin **SEO** section | Per-store SEO and redirects for merchants |
| **Email templates** | Admin **Settings** > **Email Templates** | Per-store email templates |
| **Feature flags and app settings** | Admin **Settings** (General, Theme, Feature Flags) | Redesigned platform settings |
| **Analytics and system statistics** | Admin **Analytics**, stat cards in the admin area | Redesigned platform analytics |

## Coming soon: smaller gaps

- **Two-factor authentication at sign-in.** Sign-in will ask for your code. Until then, turning
  2FA on does not add a check at sign-in. It is a required feature, but not a top priority right
  now. Account recovery for a lost phone is also not available yet.
- **A support contact.** A support contact is coming soon.

## Changing during the rebuild

These work today but **may change**:

- **Billing** moves from your personal account to your organisation.
- **Plan features and prices** on the Pricing page will be replaced with real StoreCart limits.
- **Deleting your account** will take organisations and stores into account.
- **Admin tools** for suspending users, managing admin roles and cancelling subscriptions are
  planned; the audit log gains filters by organisation, store, user and action.
- **Profile photo upload** is not working yet.
- The **Contact** page form is disabled because it does not send messages yet. No placeholder
  contact details are shown. A support contact is coming soon.
