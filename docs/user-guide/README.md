# StoreCart user guide

StoreCart is a commerce platform for merchants. When it is complete, you will sign up, create an
organisation, open several stores under it, and sell through each store's own storefront.

> **Status: early release.** StoreCart is being rebuilt on a new foundation. Today you can create an
> account, manage your profile and security, and choose a subscription plan. Stores, products,
> storefronts and orders are **not available yet**. See [What's coming](roadmap.md).
>
> Some screens still show features that are being removed or redesigned. This guide marks them
> clearly. If a screen differs from this guide, the screen may have changed during the rebuild.

## Who this guide is for

| You are... | Start here |
|---|---|
| New to StoreCart | [Getting started](getting-started.md) |
| A merchant with an account | [Your dashboard](dashboard.md), then [Profile, settings and security](profile-and-settings.md) |
| The person who pays for StoreCart | [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md) |
| A StoreCart platform administrator (StoreCart staff only) | [Admin guide](admin-guide.md) |
| Curious about what's next | [What's coming](roadmap.md) |

## Pages in this guide

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): create an account, verify your email, sign in and out,
   and reset a forgotten password.
2. [Your dashboard](dashboard.md): what the dashboard shows and how to get around.
3. [Profile, settings and security](profile-and-settings.md): your profile, preferences, password,
   two-factor authentication, login history, and deleting your account.
4. [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md): plans, upgrading, changing or cancelling, invoices
   and payment methods.
5. [Admin guide](admin-guide.md): for platform administrators only.
6. [What's coming](roadmap.md): commerce features on the way, and features that were removed for
   redesign.

## How to read the labels in this guide

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Available** | Works today. |
| **May change** | You can see it on screen, but it may look or behave differently after the rebuild. Don't rely on it yet. |
| **Being removed** | Visible today but taken out of this release. It will come back later in a redesigned form. |
| **Coming soon** | Not built yet. |

## Getting help

The **Contact** page's message form may not send messages yet, and its contact details are
placeholders (**may change**). A support channel will be announced before launch.
