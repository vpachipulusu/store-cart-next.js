# Getting started

**Who this is for:** anyone creating or using a StoreCart account.

This page covers creating an account, verifying your email, signing in and out, and resetting a
forgotten password.

## Create an account

You can sign up with an email and password, or with your Google or GitHub account.

### With email and password

1. Go to the **Sign up** page (or choose **Sign up** on the sign-in page).
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**.
   - Your password must be at least 8 characters long and contain an uppercase letter, a
     lowercase letter and a number.
3. Select **Create Account**.
4. You are taken to the **Verify your email** page. Continue with
   [Verify your email](#verify-your-email).

### With Google or GitHub

1. On the **Sign up** page, select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**.
2. Approve the sign-in in the window that opens.
3. You are taken to your dashboard.

### About plans at sign-up

Every new account starts on the **Free** plan.

If you reached the sign-up page from the Pricing page, you may see a "Selected Plan" badge. This
badge does **not** upgrade you or charge you. To move to a paid plan, sign in and follow
[Upgrade to a paid plan](plans-and-billing.md#upgrade-to-a-paid-plan).

## Verify your email

You need to verify your email address before you can use your dashboard. Until you do, StoreCart
sends you back to the **Verify your email** page.

1. Open the email we sent to the address you signed up with.
2. Select the verification link in that email.
3. Return to StoreCart and select **I've verified my email**.
4. You are taken to your dashboard.

If you can't find the email:

1. Check your spam or junk folder.
2. On the **Verify your email** page, select **Resend verification email**.
3. If you signed up with the wrong address, select **Create a new account** and sign up again.

If you see "Email not verified yet", the link has not been opened yet. Open it, then try again.

## Sign in

1. Go to the **Login** page ("Welcome back").
2. Do one of the following:
   - Enter your **Email** and **Password**, then select **Sign In**.
   - Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**.
3. You are taken to your dashboard.

> **Two-factor authentication:** if you turned on two-factor authentication, StoreCart may ask for
> a code from your authenticator app when you sign in. How and when this prompt appears **may
> change** during the rebuild. See [Two-factor authentication](profile-and-settings.md#two-factor-authentication-2fa).

## Sign out

1. Select your avatar in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
2. Select **Log out**.

You are signed out and returned to the sign-in page.

## Reset a forgotten password

Use this if you signed up with an email and password and can't remember it.

1. On the **Login** page, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **Email** and select **Send Reset Link**.
3. You see "Password reset email sent!". Open the email and select the link.
4. Choose a new password on the page that opens.
5. Return to StoreCart and sign in with your new password.

Tips:

- If no email arrives, check your spam folder and confirm you used the address you signed up with.
- If you signed up with Google or GitHub, you don't have a StoreCart password. Sign in with that
  provider instead.
- If you know your current password and just want a new one, use
  [Change your password](profile-and-settings.md#change-your-password) instead.
